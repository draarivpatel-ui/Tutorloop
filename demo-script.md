# TutorLoop: demo video shot list and narration

**For:** Next Founders Hackathon (https://next-founders.devpost.com/). The rules ask for "a 5 minute video" covering The Problem, The Build, The Demo, and Scalability, judged 25% each on Technical Execution, Innovation & UX, Business & Finance, and Communication.
**Target length: 4:45.** That leaves 15 seconds of slack under the 5:00 limit. Narration is about 620 words (timings re-cut Oct 10 for the quick-start shot; time a practice take), timed at ~140 words per minute plus a few seconds per demo shot for clicks. If a take runs long, cut the lines marked *(cut if long)* first.

## Before you hit record
1. Open **https://aariv.me/Tutorloop/** in a fresh incognito window so it starts empty. Zoom the browser to 110–125%.
2. Have open in other tabs or windows: the GitHub repo (https://github.com/draarivpatel-ui/Tutorloop) with `js/core.js` visible, a terminal in the repo folder, the README "Scalability" section, and https://aariv.me/Tutorloop/#about.
3. In the terminal, run `npm test` and `npm run test:dom` once beforehand so you know they pass (as of Oct 11, ~7:45 PM ET: **64 passed** plus the service-worker check, **45 DOM checks passed**; the headless-Chromium suite printed **167 passed** and the print check **32 passed**). Only mention the browser suite if `npm run test:e2e` passes on your machine the day you record, and quote the number it prints. *Before this update the script said 47 / 30, so the submitted video (youtu.be/sluzHYB9NhU) may quote those older counts; don't re-record just for this. These numbers are for any re-record.*
4. Shot 3 starts from an **empty** app (fresh incognito). Demo numbers are fixed relative to today, so after **Load sample data** you will always see: **$115 unpaid**, **Ethan Brooks owes $90** (2 sessions), **Maya Chen owes $25**, **2.25 of 10 service hours**. The sample data has exactly 3 students, which is the free-plan limit, so adding a 4th shows the upgrade wall.
5. Record with OBS, Loom, or QuickTime. A face-cam bubble in the corner helps the Communication score. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**.

## Optional 10-second beat: part payments (not in the submitted video; don't re-record it)
Use it in a live walkthrough or Q&A if a judge asks "what if a parent only pays part?". With sample data loaded: **Invoices** (pick Ethan) → **Record a payment** card → type an amount smaller than the balance (e.g. 10) → **Record payment**. Say: "A parent sent part of it on Venmo. I record it; it covers the oldest sessions first, the rest stays as a part payment, and Who owes you now says, for example, '$10.00 of $25.00 paid'." Then glance at the family statement in **Summary**: it lists "Payments received in <month>". Amounts must be positive and not more than the balance; the form refuses anything else.

## Things to keep true on camera
- Say "demo checkout" for plan upgrades; no payment is processed.
- One-click reminders are built (they open your email app). Scheduled/automatic reminders, cloud sync, and the Club sponsor dashboard are roadmap. Don't show or imply them as built.
- "Works offline" means after the first visit.
- Sample students (Maya, Ethan, Sofia) are fictional demo data. While they're loaded, the bar at the top says so, and **Start fresh with my own students** clears them (undoable). If you end a take on an app page, you can click it to show a clean start.
- On the "Who pays" section, the $1.6 million line is a ceiling if every NHS chapter paid, not a forecast. Say "ceiling".
- If you interview any real tutors before recording, add one true sentence to shot 2. Don't invent a quote.
